Why I ended up with it

I don't want these reviews to begin with a specification sheet. I want them to begin with the reason the thing came home in the first place. What problem was I trying to solve? What was I using before? And after enough days outside, did it actually solve that problem?

This is where your real story would continue: the first outing, the conditions that exposed something unexpected, the day you stopped babying it, and the point where you knew whether it was staying.

What matters after the honeymoon

Long-term use is where this site can be different. Scratches, repairs, fit issues, wet days, cold mornings and repeated packing tell a much better story than an afternoon with a brand-new product. This section can mix narrative with the technical details that actually matter instead of forcing every review into a giant specification table.

Would I buy it again?

Yes—or no, when that's the answer. This is the closing recommendation and the place to explain value in plain language.
