Matt in the Mountains — Concept 08

This pass carries the spatial/editorial motion language beyond Journal, adds a four-story homepage journal feed, increases the large JOURNAL ghost type contrast, removes the persistent cursor halo, and rebuilds Photography as an uncropped masonry-style gallery that preserves each image's native composition.

Logo experiment 04 uses two heavily overlapping chunky M forms with a small negative-space/snow-notch detail to recover the mountain reading while keeping MM legibility.

Original photography is not edited or rewritten. Layout, scaling, parallax and responsive presentation happen in the browser only.
