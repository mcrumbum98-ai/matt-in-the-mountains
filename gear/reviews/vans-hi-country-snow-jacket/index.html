THE VERDICT / TL;DR

Vans Hi-Country 3L Snow Jacket

Worth every penny if you can find it cheap but don't bother paying full price.

Pros

  • Water repellant
  • Large front zip pockets

Cons

  • Zipper malfunction
  • No vents

Testing the Jacket

This jacket is a tank when it comes to repelling water. I have worn it on the wettest rainiest ski days and not felt a single drop on me.

  • Waterproof coating
  • Large zip pockets for climbing skins
  • Jacket rolls into hood nicely

Testing

There is no such thing as bad weather, only bad clothing
Worth Knowing

The main zipper on mine has been regularly getting stuck at the bottom, leading to awkward escapes from the jacket and fighting with it to zip back up.
